France has officially increased the minimum financial proof required for non-EU international students, and the new figure applies from this Saturday, 1 August. We wanted you to hear it from us, with the numbers laid out clearly, so you can plan properly.

Who this applies to
✓  New students applying for a French student visa from outside the EU, EEA or Switzerland
✓  International students already in France renewing a residence permit under student status
If you are applying from India, this applies to you.
One thing worth knowing
The requirement is now linked to the French minimum wage (SMIC). That means it can be adjusted automatically whenever the SMIC is revised, rather than staying fixed for years. Plan with a small buffer above the minimum.

What this means for you

If you are planning to apply for a French student visa, update your financial planning now and make sure your proof of funds meets the new figure before you submit. Getting this right the first time is one of the most common reasons applications move smoothly or get delayed.
